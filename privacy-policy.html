Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how vexx3m.github.io collects, uses, and safeguards information when you visit and enjoy our free web gaming services.

Last Updated: October 2026 GDPR, CCPA & COPPA Compliant Zero Personal Data Sold

1. Introduction

Welcome to vexx3m.github.io (“we”, “our”, or “the Site”). We respect the privacy of our visitors and are deeply committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with international data protection standards, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

By accessing or playing games on vexx3m.github.io, you agree to the practices outlined in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please discontinue the use of our services.

2. Information We Collect

We adhere to the principle of data minimization. We do not require user registration, account creation, or payment credentials to play our games.

A. Automatically Collected Technical Data

When you visit the Site, our web servers and Google Analytics may log standard non-personally identifiable technical information:

  • Browser & Device Information: Browser type, operating system version, screen resolution, and language settings.
  • Log Information: Anonymized IP addresses, date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and session duration.
  • Gameplay Preferences: Game progress, unlocked levels, and high scores stored locally in your browser’s localStorage or IndexedDB. We never transmit your local game saves to remote servers.

B. Voluntary Information

If you contact us through our contact form or via email, we collect only the name, email address, and message contents you provide to respond to your inquiry.

3. Cookies & Web Beacons

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that enable websites to recognize returning browsers and optimize performance:

  • Essential Functional Cookies: Necessary to remember your UI preferences, volume controls, and dark theme toggles.
  • Analytics Cookies (Google Analytics 4): Measure aggregate traffic trends, popular games, and load times to help us improve user experience. All IP addresses are anonymized.
  • Advertising Cookies (Google AdSense & Partners): Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to our website or other websites on the internet.

Google DART Cookie: Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings or via aboutads.info.

4. Children’s Online Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Protecting the privacy of children is of paramount importance to us. vexx3m.github.io operates in compliance with COPPA:

  • We do not knowingly collect, store, or solicit personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
  • No user accounts, profiles, chat rooms, or public commenting features are enabled on our platform.
  • If a parent or guardian discovers that their child has provided us with personal contact information, please notify us immediately at contact.vexx3m@gmail.com, and we will promptly purge the data from our records.

5. Your Data Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have fundamental rights regarding your digital footprint:

  • Right of Access & Portability: You may request confirmation on whether any data related to you is processed.
  • Right to Erasure (“Right to Be Forgotten”): You may request the deletion of any personal communications sent to our email.
  • Right to Restrict or Object: You can configure your browser to reject all third-party cookies or activate “Do Not Track” (DNT) signals.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will never deny or degrade your gaming access for exercising any privacy rights.

6. Third-Party Game Embeds & Links

Our catalog contains embedded HTML5 games hosted in isolated iframes. While we thoroughly inspect each source for malware, third-party game creators may independently utilize their own analytics or sound storage. We encourage users to review the privacy statements of any third-party services linked from games.
