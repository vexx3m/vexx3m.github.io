Getaway Shootout Description

Getaway Shootout is a wildly chaotic physics racing shooter. Race 3 other contestants across perilous obstacles to reach the getaway helicopter or van first, picking up rocket launchers, snipers, and power-ups along the way!

  • Unpredictable two-button physics jumping mechanics.
  • Hilarious local 2-player competitive multiplayer mode.
  • Randomized weapons: shotguns, sniper rifles, bombs, and shields.
  • Dynamic getaway stages from subway stations to rooftops.
