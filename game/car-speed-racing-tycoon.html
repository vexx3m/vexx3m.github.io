Controls / How to Play:

  • W or ↑ : Accelerate

Car Speed Racing Tycoon Description

Car Speed Racing Tycoon is an addictive automotive business and racing game available to play online for free on vex-5.github.io. Build your race track empire, buy and merge supercars, optimize your pit lane income, and take the wheel in competitive speedway races!

  • Generate passive income as your fleet laps the track, then reinvest in upgrades and facilities.

  • No download required — manage and race your dream fleet directly in your browser.
