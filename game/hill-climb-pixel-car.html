About & Controls

Controls / How to Play:

  • D or → : Gas / Accelerate / Lean Forward
  • A or ← : Brake / Reverse / Lean Backward
  • Space : Jump / Boost (when unlocked)
  • R : Restart Stage

Hill Climb Pixel Car Description

Hill Climb Pixel Car is a charming retro 2D physics-based off-road driving game, playable for free on vex-5.github.io. Guide your pixelated vehicle over steep hills, treacherous valleys, and bouncy bridges while carefully managing fuel and throttle!

  • Fun physics gameplay: balance your vehicle in mid-air to land smoothly on steep downhill slopes.
  • Collect golden coins and fuel canisters to keep your engine running and buy powerful upgrades.
  • Unlock an awesome lineup of pixel vehicles including buggies, 4x4 trucks, and monster machines.
  • No download required — enjoy addictive retro driving on any device.
