DMCA & Copyright Policy

vexx3m.github.io is dedicated to respecting intellectual property rights and complying with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512).

24-Hour Takedown Resolution Dedicated Copyright Agent Supporting Indie Developers

1. Commitment to Copyright Compliance

vexx3m.github.io operates as an online content aggregator, indexer, and portal for free web-based HTML5 games. We deeply appreciate and respect the creative endeavors of game developers, artists, and sound composers.

The majority of games showcased on this website are distributed freely by their original authors for promotional, viral, or ad-supported web distribution. It is our strict policy to respond expeditiously to legitimate infringement notices pursuant to the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA).

2. How to Submit a DMCA Takedown Notice

If you are the copyright owner or an authorized agent acting on behalf of a rights holder, and believe that any game or material on vexx3m.github.io infringes upon your copyright, please submit a written notification containing the following details:

  1. Identification of the Work: A clear description of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed (or a representative list if multiple works are involved).
  2. Specific Location (URL): The exact URL(s) on vexx3m.github.io where the allegedly infringing material is hosted or embedded.
  3. Your Contact Information: Your full legal name, company name (if applicable), mailing address, telephone number, and email address.
  4. Good Faith Statement: A statement that you have a good faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  5. Accuracy & Perjury Statement: A statement that the information in the notification is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, that you are authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.
  6. Physical or Electronic Signature: An electronic or physical signature of the person authorized to act on behalf of the copyright holder.

3. Designated Copyright Agent Contact

Please email your formal DMCA notice directly to our designated copyright desk:

Designated Agent Email: contact.vexx3m@gmail.com
Subject Line: DMCA Takedown Notice - [Game Title]
Expected Turnaround Time: Within 24 hours of verified receipt.

4. Counter-Notification Procedure

If a game provided by a developer is removed as a result of a DMCA notice and the provider believes this was done in error or misidentification, they may submit a counter-notification under 17 U.S.C. § 512(g). Upon receiving a valid counter-notice, we will forward it to the original complaining party.

5. Game Developer Attribution & Partnerships

Are you a game developer seeking wider distribution, player exposure, or proper attribution links? We are proud to feature developer credits, links to your official itch.io or Steam store pages, and promotional banners. Reach out via our Contact Us page anytime!
